Privacy Policy for FreeTV

Effective Date: 2 October 2026

FreeTV (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and Android TV application FreeTV handles user information.

1. About FreeTV

FreeTV is a free live TV streaming directory and aggregation application that helps users discover publicly available and free-to-air live TV streams from different sources around the world.

The app allows users to search and browse channels, filter channels by source, category, language, live status, sports, soccer, cricket, Hindi, English and other available categories. Users may also preview available streams, manage favorites, and access channel information.

FreeTV does not host, upload, reproduce, or modify third-party video content. Available streams are provided by their respective public or third-party sources. When a user opens a stream, playback may occur through the applicable third-party streaming service or source.

2. Information We Collect

❌ Personal Information

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Account passwords

✅ Non-Personal Information

We may collect or process limited, non-personal information automatically to improve app performance and reliability, such as:

  • Device type
  • Android version
  • App version
  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Basic technical information required to operate the application

This information is intended to be used for technical and operational purposes and is not intended to personally identify you.

3. App Preferences and Favorites

FreeTV may allow users to save preferences such as favorite channels, selected filters, or other app settings.

Where such information is stored locally on your device, it remains on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to us as personally identifiable information.

4. Use of Information

Any information collected or processed by FreeTV may be used only for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the application
  • Improving app stability and performance
  • Diagnosing and fixing crashes and technical problems
  • Improving the user experience
  • Improving channel discovery and filtering functionality

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share personal information with third parties.

5. Third-Party Streaming Sources

FreeTV may provide access to publicly available live streams supplied by third-party websites, broadcasters, streaming providers, or other public sources.

FreeTV does not claim ownership of third-party content and does not control the privacy practices, availability, advertisements, cookies, tracking technologies, or data collection practices of external streaming providers.

When you access a third-party stream or website, the privacy policy and terms of that third party may apply.

Users should review the privacy policies of the respective third-party services before using their services.

6. Content and Streaming Disclaimer

FreeTV is intended to help users discover publicly available and free-to-air streaming sources.

The application does not guarantee the availability, accuracy, legality, quality, or continued operation of any third-party stream.

Third-party channels and streams may be added, removed, changed, or become unavailable without notice.

FreeTV does not intentionally host or store third-party video content on its own servers unless explicitly stated otherwise.
